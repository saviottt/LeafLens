About PlantCare AI

Empowering growers and farmers with accessible, artificial intelligence diagnostics.

Our Mission

PlantCare AI was developed to bridge the critical gap between agricultural science and field farming. Crop diseases destroy up to 40% of global food supplies each year. By leveraging advanced computer vision and multimodal artificial intelligence, PlantCare AI equips farmers, agronomists, and home gardeners with fast, accessible preliminary assessments directly from an ordinary smartphone photo.

How the AI Detection Works

When you submit a photograph of an affected plant, the system analyzes the visual patterns using advanced multimodal machine learning models:

  • Image Verification: The system verifies that the image depicts plant matter, filtering out unrelated photos.
  • Plant & Tissue Identification: Identifies the crop species and affected organ (leaf, stem, root, or fruit).
  • Pathological Feature Extraction: Detects symptomatic patterns such as chlorosis, necrotic lesions, fungal spores, mosaic mottling, and wilting.
  • Structured Health Analysis: Generates a structured diagnosis with severity estimation, actionable cultural controls, and long-term prevention strategies.

Best Practices for Photo Capture

For the highest diagnostic accuracy, keep these tips in mind when photographing your crops:

Natural Daytime Lighting

Photograph during daylight hours. Avoid intense camera flash glare or harsh shadows that obscure leaf texture.

Sharp Focus

Ensure the camera focuses directly on the affected lesion or discoloration rather than the background soil.

Show the Symptom Boundary

Capture the margin where healthy tissue meets diseased tissue — this provides critical diagnostic context.

Frequently Asked Questions

Agricultural & Medical Disclaimer

Important Advisory: PlantCare AI is an assistive decision-support application built for educational and preliminary guidance purposes only. Machine learning models can make errors or misclassify rare pathogens and abiotic stresses.

Under no circumstances should recommendations generated by this software substitute for in-person inspection and laboratory pathology testing conducted by a certified agronomist or local agricultural extension authority. Always consult official regional pest management guidelines and strictly observe protective safety instructions before applying any agrochemical sprays or fertilizers.
